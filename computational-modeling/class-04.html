The peak is one sample in a swing of hundreds of m/s² every few tenths of a millisecond.

Step 1 of 7 · What peak does the file give as it is?

what we foundFour milliseconds, every sample The record goes from −964 at 27.2 ms to +28 at 27.4 ms, and up to +512 at 28.6 ms.
what we learnA fast feature It is hard to picture the whole car reversing its deceleration that quickly. The swings could be the structure ringing or the sensor's mount, and nothing in the file tells the two apart.
nextRemove the fast content The customer will probably filter it the way the standard says, so we need that filter first.

Each output is built from three inputs and the two outputs before it.

Step 2 of 7 · How do we build the standard's filter?

$$y_i = a_0 x_i + a_1 x_{i-1} + a_2 x_{i-2} + b_1 y_{i-1} + b_2 y_{i-2}$$ $$\omega_d = 2\pi \cdot \textcolor{#8A6508}{\mathrm{CFC}} \cdot 2.0775 \qquad \omega_a = \tan\frac{\omega_d \textcolor{#8A6508}{T}}{2} \qquad D = 1 + \sqrt{2}\,\omega_a + \omega_a^2$$ $$a_0 = a_2 = \frac{\omega_a^2}{D} \qquad a_1 = 2a_0 \qquad b_1 = \frac{-2(\omega_a^2 - 1)}{D} \qquad b_2 = \frac{-1 + \sqrt{2}\,\omega_a - \omega_a^2}{D}$$ $$\textcolor{#8A6508}{\text{CFC} = 60,\ T = 0.0001\text{ s}}: \qquad a_0 = a_2 = 0.00145237 \qquad a_1 = 0.00290473$$ $$b_1 = 1.88934322 \qquad b_2 = -0.89515269$$ $$\frac{a_0 + a_1 + a_2}{1 - b_1 - b_2} = \frac{0.00580947}{0.00580947} = 1 \qquad \text{a constant passes through unchanged}$$

J211 Appendix C, as in lecture 12. The Civic (test 9808) records 10,000 samples a second, so $T$ = 0.0001 s.

One curve is CFC 60 run forward only, and the other runs forward and then backward.

if they compare carsCFC 60 A peak sent to a customer is most likely set beside their own numbers for another car (total vehicle comparison).

Step 5 of 7 · How much does the filter change the answer?

what we found−964.0 at 27.2 ms becomes −355.5 at 62.4 ms As recorded, then CFC 60 forward and backward. The filtered peak is 2.7 times smaller.
what we learnThe two peaks are different moments of the crash What comes off the top of a narrow spike goes into its shoulders. At 27.2 ms CFC 60 reads −145.4, and a broader dip later becomes the largest. Forward then backward has no lag, so the later peak is not a delay.

Step 7 of 7 · Does the answer make sense?

the filterA constant comes back as 1 An array of ones filtered at CFC 60 returns ones, to 13 decimal places. The filter removes nothing from a constant, as a low-pass filter must.
the rate10 000 is above 249 The coefficients exist only above 4.155 times the class, which is 249 samples a second for CFC 60.
the startZero or the first sample Starting each pass from the first sample instead of from zero gives the same peak, since the record starts near zero.
the offset−0.043 m/s² Removing the mean before contact moves the peak by less than 0.01 m/s². The customer will probably filter the file as it is, so we did too.
what we concludeOf the choices we checked, filtering at all moves the answer most The start and the offset move it by less than the last digit we report, one pass moves it by 4.5%, and CFC 60 against the raw record by a factor of 2.7.

Each step left a choice that your report should state.

A reported peak or level usually needs its filter beside it before anyone can check it.

a noisy workplaceSound in dBA
A sound level meter weights each frequency by how well the ear hears it. OSHA's 8-hour limit is 90 dBA "at slow response," which is a filter in time as well, and a reading on another weighting cannot be checked against it.
a wildfire-smoke weekPM2.5 against a 24-hour standard
EPA's 24-hour standard for fine particles is 35 µg/m³, and it is compared with a 24-hour average (red). The hourly readings (gray) reach well above that average, and a report has to say which of the two it gives.
a bike computerTop speed from GPS
The same ride gives two top speeds, one from each second's fix and one from a 10-second average. Both are correct, so a reported top speed should say which window it used.

The weighting curve is the one in IEC 61672-1. The smoke week and the ride are made up.
