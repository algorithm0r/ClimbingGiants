How they work

  • Meet at least one hour every teaching week, in person or by video
  • Each week, assign a moderator (time, agenda, everyone speaks) and a minutes taker
  • Each group has a room on Discord

  • Missed a meeting? It counts as late, not missing: read the minutes and confirm

The courseWhat we work on

Crash tests

  • NHTSA frontal barrier tests at about 56 km/h
  • Your own test, from a pool of 197
  • Eight channels at 10, 12.5 or 20 kHz

Basketball shots

  • Ball position, tracked frame by frame
  • A training set and three test files
  • Assignment 5 and most extra problems

Slope stability lab (optional)

  • An extra labor on a GPU simulator in my lab
  • Your own slope, 40 runs
  • Stages in weeks 4, 7 and 10

Methods: integration and differentiation of sampled data · filtering to a standard · least squares · root finding · time stepping · uncertainty.

How it runsDeliverables and grading

Four each teaching week, 36 in all

  • Assignment · a report (PDF) and the program that produced it
  • Quiz · in Canvas
  • Reflection · from your learning log
  • Meeting · your group's minutes

Complete or incomplete

  • Complete: the method fits, it is applied correctly, the number is defensible, the choices are stated
  • Incomplete work comes back with a comment, and you resubmit (late after the due date)

The request

"There's crash data in that zip and some other stuff. I need a thorough report on what this data is before we can produce our analysis." · Alice and Bob

  • Plots · each says what it shows, of what, in what units
  • Errors · anything wrong is called out, with your evidence
  • Assumptions · every decision the data did not make for you

Extra problem: Bob's basketball data, +0.1.

What every report needs

  • The answer in the first paragraph
  • Your phrase at the top, your program submitted with it
  • Written for yourself a year ago
  • The style is yours: a memo, a technical report and a notebook are on the example reports page

Assignment 1Getting started · live

The steps

  1. Your data · roll a phrase, declare it, download the pack
  2. VS Code guide · open the pack, run the program, read the channel list
  3. Workbench · plot any channel with no code
  4. Example reports · three finished reports for this assignment

The demo uses held-back test 9808, which no phrase assigns.

What to look for in your file

  • Units that cannot be right
  • Labels that say more than the header does
  • The time axis, built from t0, dt and n
  • Channels your test does not have
