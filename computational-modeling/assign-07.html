Assignment 6 · The Head Injury Criterion

Submit your report as a PDF to the Canvas assignment by midnight on the due date, together with the program that produced it. Write your four-word data phrase at the top of the first page. Remember to cite all sources other than the text, course material, or your own notes.

Problem

Alice, back from Detroit:

The customer wants the head injury criterion for this test, computed on the resultant of the three head channels. Their engineers will check it against the regulation, so it has to arrive with the limit we used and where that limit comes from.

I need the number. I also need to know how many windows you had to look at to get it, because there are two hundred more tests behind this one and I would like to know what I am committing to.

The report is held to the same three standards as assignment 1: plots that can be identified at a glance, anything that looks wrong flagged, and every assumption you had to make written down.

Alice is costing out a month of work, so she needs:

  • The number.
  • The limit you used, and where it comes from.
  • How many windows you examined, against how many there would have been without the limit.
  • What the search cost, and what two hundred of them would cost.

Please see the example reports for three reports on an example pack, each in a different style. The Example reports page says what every report needs and who you are writing it for.

If you do not know where to start
  • The resultant of three channels is the square root of the sum of their squares, sample by sample.
  • Every admissible window of the record is a candidate. A running total computed once turns each window’s integral into one subtraction. Windowed optimum covers it.
  • For Bob: the short records stop long before the ball reaches the hoop, so the crossing has to come from the model and not from the samples.
  • For Bob: a falling parabola passes through any height below its peak twice. Conditions as roots and Root-finding failures cover which one a solver hands back.
  • Reading the assignment 6 program goes through this assignment's example program a section at a time, with notes on its lines that link to the Reading Python pages.

Extra Problem

This is worth +0.1 and can be submitted any time before the end of the quarter without penalty.

Bob:

Now the flight model works, I want to know where the ball is when it crosses the height of the rim. Use the short records, the ones that stop a few frames after the release. Get me the time and the place on a dozen of them.

Tell me how you know it is the right crossing.

Bob wants:

  • The time of each crossing, and where the ball is when it happens.
  • How you know it is the crossing he means.
