State as a vector, for a tracked shot and a belted occupant

Carrying the velocity in the state turns one second-order equation into a pair of first-order ones.

r is the position (x, y, z), v the velocity, |v| the speed, g = 9.81 m/s², ẑ points up, and k = 0.016 per meter for a basketball.

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}^2\mathbf{r}}{\mathrm{d}t^2} = -g\,\hat{\mathbf{z}} - k\,|\mathbf{v}|\,\mathbf{v} \qquad\longrightarrow\qquad \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{r}\\ \mathbf{v}\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{v}\\ \textcolor{#8A6508}{-g\,\hat{\mathbf{z}} - k\,|\mathbf{v}|\,\mathbf{v}}\end{bmatrix}$$

The state holds everything the rate needs, six numbers for the shot.

$$\begin{aligned} |\mathbf{v}| &= \sqrt{4.7333^2 + 3.9732^2 + 2.7508^2} = 6.764\ \text{m/s} \\[6pt] \frac{\mathrm{d}v_x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= -k\,|\mathbf{v}|\,v_x = -0.016 \times 6.764 \times (-4.7333) = \textcolor{#8A6508}{0.512\ \text{m/s}^2} \end{aligned}$$

The shot’s physics is one function, and the step takes any rate function and state.

The step works on a state of any length, because + and * act on every entry of a NumPy array.

any rate function f, any state s

Euler, on every entry at once

A belted occupant is a state of two, driven by the car’s recorded acceleration.

u is the occupant’s travel relative to the car and w its rate. m = 75 kg is the occupant. k = 296 kN/m and c = 2.8 kN·s/m are the belt. acar is NHTSA test 10642, a 2019 BMW X5 into a barrier, from a rear crossmember at 12,500 samples a second.

$$m\ddot u + c\dot u + k u = -m\,a_{\text{car}}(t) \qquad\longrightarrow\qquad \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\begin{bmatrix}u\\ w\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}w\\[4pt] (-c\,w - k\,u)/m \textcolor{#8A6508}{\;-\;a_{\text{car}}(t)}\end{bmatrix}$$

The occupant’s rate uses the time, to look up the car.

tc and ac are the record’s times and accelerations. The step is the same function as the shot’s.
