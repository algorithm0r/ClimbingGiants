What decides the class

The right class is set by what the measured thing responds to.

That range of frequencies is its bandwidth.

The class comes from what the number is for, outside the record.

as recordedAlready filtered The lab's 2,000 Hz filter ran before sampling.

SAE J211 fixes the class in advance

SAE J211 assigns a class to each measurement.

CFC 1000Head, for HIC The head responds to fast content (HIC, the head injury criterion).
CFC 180Chest The chest responds mostly below a few hundred hertz.
CFC 180Structure, for velocity Smooths the record and changes Δv by under 0.02%.
CFC 60Belt and barrier loads Load cells vibrate at their own frequency, and CFC 60 removes that vibration.

The physics sets the class roughly, and J211 fixes the exact number.

the physicsNot 60 for a head CFC 60 removes the content HIC is computed from.
the convention1000, not 900 or 1100 Either would likely work as well, so J211 picks one.
the payoffComparable numbers Two labs testing the same car can compare results.
